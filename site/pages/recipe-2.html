Spinach and feta pie

Jump to recipe, print recipe or save it to your favourites for later.

The trick is to cook the aromatics slowly so they turn sweet. This is the recipe I make when friends come over on short notice.

You can prepare most of it the night before and finish it in minutes.

Ingredients

  • 3 cloves garlic, minced
  • 1 lemon, zested and juiced
  • salt and black pepper to taste
  • 1 cup vegetable stock
  • 1 teaspoon smoked paprika
  • 250 grams arborio rice
  • 2 ripe bananas, mashed
  • 1 large onion, finely chopped

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a heavy pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the onion and cook for eight minutes until soft.
  3. Stir in the garlic and spices and cook for one more minute.
  4. Pour in the tomatoes and stock, then bring to a gentle simmer.
  5. Cover and cook for twenty minutes, stirring now and then.
  6. Fold in the spinach and lemon juice just before serving.
  7. Taste, adjust the seasoning and let it rest for five minutes.
